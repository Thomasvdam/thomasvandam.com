# Scorekeeper artwork

The scorekeeper's visual direction takes inspiration from the blue sea, sunset,
gold lettering, and parchment cards in the publisher's
[product photography](https://www.999games.nl/product/1000-bommen-granaten/8719214424725).
The ship illustration is original generated artwork, rather than extracted box art.

Asset: `public/images/bommen-en-granaten/pirate-seas.webp` (1536 × 1024, about 341 KiB).
Generated using the built-in imagegen tool; encoded as WebP with Sharp at quality 84.
The title uses the self-hosted Pirata One font via `next/font/google`. Body text
and numeric scores retain the site's existing fonts for readability.

## Generation prompt

Create an original richly painted pirate adventure illustration for a board-game scorekeeper website. Landscape panoramic composition, 1536x1024 or similar. A wooden pirate brig with billowing black sails bearing a cream skull and crossed bones occupies the RIGHT third, sailing toward the viewer through deep luminous cobalt blue ocean waves. Golden orange sunset on the LEFT horizon behind distant tropical islands, dramatic navy blue clouds at top, warm rim lighting on rigging and wood, a small red pennant. Art direction: classic European family board game box illustration, expressive detailed brushwork, adventurous and warm, realistic illustrative rendering with saturated jewel blues and amber, parchment-era pirate fantasy, inviting not scary. Leave the left third mostly atmospheric sky and sea with simple shapes for overlaid website title. No lettering, no text, no logos, no watermark, no frame. This is a website background illustration, NOT a picture of a box, no UI, no cards or dice.
